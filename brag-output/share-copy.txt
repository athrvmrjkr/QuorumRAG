### 💼 LinkedIn Post
Excited to share Quorum AI, an enterprise-grade multi-agent RAG research platform built to solve the hallucination and latency bottlenecks common in production retrieval systems.

Instead of relying on heavy frameworks like LangChain, Quorum uses a lightweight custom Python orchestrator coordinating four specialized agents:
1. Input Domain Guardrail (blocks off-topic compute waste)
2. Cloud Vector Retrieval (Qdrant HNSW cosine index)
3. Academic Synthesizer (streaming LaTeX derivations at 300+ tok/s)
4. Anti-Hallucination Critic (verifies every claim against source pages with automated threshold overrides)
